**Red Egg**: pulsating, nourishing/euphoric if eaten. Hatches evergrowning red slug.

**Brachia Segmentum Sample:** twists, foliage bursting, eagerly snares; metabolzing. 

**Big Eyeball:** suspended in fluid. Follows you. Crisp iris. A god wants it back.

**Dead Chip:** memory chip with roster of billions of dead, self-updates constantly.

**Lozenge:** disolves in the mouth, body slowly refracts; thinning and expanding.

**Stone Egg:** thick crust, hard to scan. Begins unfolding if cooled. Emperor's young.

**Squat Idol:** contorted features, formed in an empty stomach, leaches into water.

**Thinking Stone:** nanotech, accelerates though processes, burns out the body in the process.  

**Skeleton:** unusual structure, long many-jointed limbs, elongate skull, predates its planetoid.

**Preserved Xenoflesh:** intense sulfuric smell, still crawls w/ bacterial life: necrotic.

**Protective Mask:** many holes, inscrutibly tinted eyes, etched prayers, gas filters.

**Dream Particle:** near-limitless power, highly radioactive, spectrography reveals: language.

**Lunchbox:** packed with ash-grey love letters, written in curled script.

**Invitation:** encrypted paper card invites recipient to joyous flesh-eating.

**Final Report on Site 281:** describes bodies consumed, terrible mouths, river of blood.

**Photograph:** fuzzy outline of interstellar beast, moments before impact; recovered from wreckage.

**Jawbone;** far too many teeth, recovered from inside crewmember of the "Solidar." 

**Data Drive:** plastic-wrapped, classified, contains astrogation data for blacksite + confessions.

**Pelvis:** charred, coated in strange membrane, envelops on contact.

**Bassalt Tablet:** key to ancient vault, mildly radioactive, strange inscription: "key."

**The Lamondson Memo:** strange behavior in the baron's court, desperate experiments, cryo. 

**Package of Videotape:** 30 tapes, interior structure, dripping pillars, shrinking halls, cocoons.

**Artefact-A776:** vaccsuit made of unknown material by R. Boylan (deceased). No shadow.

**Fragment-90:** shared of xenodevice, monofilament spools; needle spines for extraction.

**Melanocephalus Elgans Embryo:** thin limbs, magnificent skull, teeth already forming.

**Object 38b:** matted ball; appears to be human hair, teeth; sound of a heartbeat.

**Object 9029 "Jester":** 4m tall wickerwork figure, preserved in cave, twitches.

**Blackbox "Fortitude":** footage of ejections, routine work, stars within, a last meal.
