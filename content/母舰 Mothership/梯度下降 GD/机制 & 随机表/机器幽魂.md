## 预骰

1. [ ] **MARINA** (NEUTRAL)  
	She was a maintenance worker assigned to the AI Core. She was the one who discovered that the warhead, meant as the ultimate failsafe against Monarch's malfunction, had been removed. Her trauma captures the final days before the facility's evacuation, the moment when the Cloudbank Synthetics Production Facility was becoming the Deep.

	The moment Marina is reliving: she went to the manager's secretary and was refused entry. The secretary told her to file a report and send it via email. Marina yelled: "I need to see the boss RIGHT NOW! Stephen confirmed it, Monarch has gone rogue. The warhead is GONE! Don't you understand what this MEANS?" The secretary called security. Marina later died in an “accident” and became a Ghost.

	A character can impersonate the manager and listen carefully to her warning, assuring her that every possible action will be taken immediately. If soothed, she will explain the details of the warhead removal, including a critical detail about an exposed Personality I/O port. If the crew acts dismissively, she will rage, use her Trauma Relay to instill fear of Monarch, and involuntarily trigger Power Drain on the surrounding electronics, growing brighter and more violent in her fury.
1. [ ] **ORBACH** (HOSTILE)  
	He was a corporate espionage agent sent by Straylight Computing System LLC into the Deep a couple of years ago, accompanied by two contractor bodyguards, Sato and Red. Mission: steal Monarch's architecture data. One of his bodyguards was replaced by an infiltrator mid-dive. Orbach is now reliving the moment when Sato killed Red and turned the gun on him.
	
	He mistakes one of the player characters for Sato and confronts them: "What the hell, you shot Red! WHY?..." A long pause. "Damn. How long, Sato? How long have you been one of THEM? Since we docked? Since we left Nebulon? Was ANY of it real?" He attacks the character with his Displacement power. He stops if the character fakes their death. He then pulls out his notepad and opens a file: "Unguarded Personality I/O port — direct access to Monarch, location: unknown."
	
	By connecting what they learn from Orbach and Marina, the crew can locate the vulnerable port and understand it can be used to harm Monarch.

2. [ ] **REEVES** (FRIENDLY)  
	She was a nineteen-year-old Troubleshooter rookie on her first deployment into the Deep. Her squad encountered Security Androids. She panicked and threw an EMP grenade without clearance. The EMP triggered a Monarch Panic: doors sealed, heat spiked, dozens of Security Androids swarmed. Nine squad mates dead in eight minutes. She died last, listening to them scream over comms.
	
	Now she is reliving the mission, convinced the player characters are her squad. Speaking softly: "There you are. Lost comms for a second. You've been conspicuous, guys. Now you do as I say and we'll get through this. We won't screw up this time. I won't let that happen."
	
	She can scout ahead, even through walls, using proper hand signals. She warns frantically when players are about to trigger Monarch. She uses surgical power drain to disable single Security Androids without triggering area-wide alerts. She remotely opens and closes doors and protects the squad with desperate competence.
	
	If players act recklessly or disobey her orders, she panics: "Contact, three Sec-Androids, southeast corridor..." She throws the grenade. "EMP OUT. Wait. No. FUCK." She emits an electromagnetic pulse that fries equipment and draws Monarch's attention.
