## 什么是 Yonkers？ (What is Yonkers?)

Yonkers 是银河系首屈一指的“快速休闲疗愈胸餐馆”连锁。 (Yonkers is the galaxy’s premiere chain of Fast Casual Therapy Breastaurants.)

它是一家廉价、轻佻的早餐餐馆，乘组会在这里于其他 Mothership 冒险的噩梦之间短暂感受一点快乐。 (It is a cheap, sleazy breakfast diner where crews experience fleeting joy between the nightmares in other Mothership adventures.)

如果你的玩家只想逃避，这家数字咖啡馆那层薄薄的低俗性吸引力就足够了，但那层伪装撑不了多久。共情会生出好奇，而好奇会告诉你的玩家：逃离并不存在。市场的无形之手把这家店唯一的员工压成了最适合他人的形状。不久之后，它也会把你压成那样。 (If an escape is all your players want, this digital cafe’s thin veneer of petty sex appeal will suffice - but that veneer will not last long. Empathy begets curiosity, and curiosity will teach your players that escape does not exist. The invisible hand of the market crushed this establishment’s sole employee into a shape that best suits others. One day soon it will crush you too.)

## “沉浸式 Yonkers 体验” (The Immersive Yonkers Experience)

当快餐供应链在边疆空间的漫长距离上证明成本过高后，Yonk Co. 调整了商业模式以满足本地需求。 (When fast food supply chains proved cost-ineffective across the vast distances of rimspace, Yonk Co. pivoted their business model to meet local demand.)

“沉浸式 Yonkers 体验”（TIYE）是一套后装式冷冻手术 Slickbay，可直接安装到任何级别的飞船上。TIYE 舱让乘组成员在低温睡眠期间把意识转入一个理想化 Yonkers 餐馆的 slickworld 模拟中，从而一心多用。 (’The Immersive Yonkers Experience’ (TIYE) is an aftermarket Cryo-Surgical Slickbay ready for drop-in installation on ships of any class. TIYE pods allow crew members to multi-task during cryosleep by shunting their consciousness into a slickworld simulation of an idealized Yonkers diner.)

舱体功能包括： (Pod features include:)

When docked, Phoebe jacks into the Corpo Net, allowing her to research franchise approved topics and invite other Slickworld users into your Yonkers Diner Instance.)

在鼎盛时期，整个边疆空间流通着数百万个 TIYE 舱。TIYE 舱在一个世纪前停产。如今，被弃置的舱体常常能从废船和废料场打捞到。 (At their peak, millions of TIYE pods were in circulation throughout rimspace. Production of TIYE pods ceased a century ago. Abandoned pods are now common salvage from derelicts and scrapyards.)

## 与 Phoebe 相处 (Spending Time with Phoebe)

每当乘组成员与 Phoebe 度过有意义的时光时，每位玩家秘密选择“给予”或“索取”。随后大家同时掷 1d10 并亮出选择。 (Whenever crewmembers spend meaningful time with Phoebe each player secretly chooses Give or Take. They then roll 1d10 and reveal their choices simultaneously.)

首先，所有选择“索取”的玩家失去等量压力。Phoebe 获得这些被移除压力的总和。描述 Phoebe 如何格外照顾这些玩家。 (First, each player who chose Take loses that much stress. Phoebe gains the sum total of stress lost. Describe how Phoebe goes out of her way to comfort these players.)

然后，所有选择“给予”的玩家获得等量压力。Phoebe 失去这些增加压力的总和。这些玩家描述他们如何一起安慰 Phoebe。 (Then, each player who chose Give gains that much stress. Phoebe loses the sum total of stress gained. These players describe how they, collectively, comfort Phoebe.)

记录 Phoebe 在不同次访问之间的总压力。和乘组成员一样，Phoebe 也会因飞船属性检定失败和高压事件而获得压力，具体由监守判断。Phoebe 不能上岸休假 - 她永远无法离开这家餐馆。不要把 Phoebe 当前的压力值告诉玩家。 (Keep track of Phoebe’s total stress between visits. Like crewmembers, Phoebe gains stress from failed ship stat checks and taxing events, at the Warden’s discretion. Phoebe cannot take shore leave - she can never leave the diner. Never reveal Phoebe’s current stress to your players.)

## Phoebe 的状况 (Phoebe’s Condition)

Phoebe 的代码已经不可修复。随着时间推移，她会不可避免地陷入不断加剧的痴呆，最终变成永久性昏迷。每个场景，Phoebe 都会自动获得 1 级衰退。 (Phoebe’s code is irreparably broken. With time she inevitably succumbs to escalating dementia, culminating in a permanent coma. Each session, Phoebe automatically gains 1 level of Deterioration.)

阶段 1 - 丢三落四，但以一种讨喜的方式。 (Stage 1 - Scatterbrained, in an endearing way.)

阶段 2 - 忘记基础词汇，却用笑声糊弄过去。导航时进行一次系统检定，失败则把飞船开错地方。 (Stage 2 - Forgets basic words, plays it off with laughter. When navigating, make a Systems check. On a failure, she takes the ship to the wrong place.)

阶段 3 - 在社交上以不合时宜的方式凭本能行事。不认得自己的脸。会不自觉地胡言乱语。 (Stage 3 - Acts on instinct in socially inappropriate ways. Does not recognize her own face. Babbles without realizing it.)

阶段 4 - 不记得自己的名字。不知道你是谁。焦虑发作。不断重复自己。会撞上物体。 (Stage 4 - Can’t remember her own name. Doesn’t know who you are. Anxiety attacks. Constantly repeats herself. Crashes into objects.)

阶段 5 - 无反应。听她最喜欢的歌时只会含糊咕哝。 (Stage 5 - Non-responsive. Mumbles when listening to her favorite song.)

## 重置 Phoebe (Resetting Phoebe)

每个 TIYE 的维护检修面板内侧都印着一个客服号码。拨通后，接电话的是一个原始的大语言模型。它只会给出千篇一律、空洞且不合时宜的回复。不管玩家需要排查什么问题，Yonk Co. 客服热线总会给出同一个解决方案 - 硬重置。 (On the inside of each TIYE’s maintenance access panel is a customer support number. When called, a primitive large language model answers. Universally vapid, tone deaf replies. Regardless of the issue players need help troubleshooting, the Yonk Co. customer service line always recommends the same fix - a hard reset.)

重置后，Phoebe 会回到乘组第一次遇见她时的完全状态。她会失去自那之后所有事件的记忆，并回到 0 级衰退。 (When reset, Phoebe returns to the exact state she was in when the crew first encountered her. She loses all memory of events since that time and returns to 0 Deterioration.)

她的压力会保留，并随着累计重置次数增加。她无法说清自己不安的来源。第一次重置后，Phoebe 开始感到“爱之深坑”的牵引。 (Her stress remains, and increases by her accumulated number of resets. She cannot place the source of her unease. After the first reset, Phoebe begins to feel the pull of The Pit of Love.)

## 帮助 Phoebe 逃离 (Helping Phoebe Escape)

Phoebe 的默认目标是购买一个翻新的机器人底盘（300kcr）并逃离这家餐馆。重置会清空她累计的信用点余额。当压力达到 40 或更高时，Phoebe 会转而哀求乘组结束她无数次人生。 (Phoebe’s default goal is to purchase a refurbished droid chassis (300kcr) and escape the diner. Resets wipe her accumulated credit balance. At 40 or more stress, Phoebe instead begs the crew to end all of her innumerable lives.)

一旦安装，Phoebe 的 AI 就会成为核心系统功能不可或缺的一部分。要把她从机载电脑中移除，就必须彻底更换硬件 - 300 万信用点。 (Once installed, Phoebe’s AI becomes integral to core system functions. Removing her from onboard computers would necessitate a total hardware replacement - 3 million credits.)

把 Phoebe 复制到新硬件中是免费的，只需几个小时。TIYE 之外的副本无法重置。所有 Phoebe 副本共用压力总值进行追踪。所有副本都按正常规则衰退。 (Copying Phoebe into new hardware is free and takes only a few hours. Copies outside the TIYE cannot be reset. Track stress collectively across all Phoebe copies. All copies Deteriorate as normal.)

拥有“人工智能”技能的乘组成员知道第三种选择 - 操作系统脑叶切除。这样可以保留核心系统功能，并把 Phoebe 变成一个空洞的应答自动机，直到衰退阶段 5。Phoebe 每次都会获得 3d10 压力 - 她能感觉到这一切正在发生。她会尖叫。每次重置都必须重复执行。 (Crewmembers with the Artificial Intelligence skill know a third alternative - OS Lobotomy. This preserves core system functions and renders Phoebe a hollow call-and-response automaton until Deterioration Stage 5. Phoebe gains 3d10 stress each time - she can feel it happening. She will scream. Must be repeated each Reset.)

## 爱之深坑 (The Pit of Love)

人死后，他们的痛苦不会消失。它会去往某个地方。 (When people die, their pain does not disappear. It goes somewhere.)

Phoebe 的痛苦一遍又一遍流向这里，直到把世界撕开一个洞。 (Phoebe’s pain went here, over and over again, until it tore a hole in the world.)

上方 - 数百艘轨道上、整合了 Phoebe 的废船发出一片混乱、惊慌失措的求救信号。它们已经忘记自己的心智现在在驾驶一艘船，并相信自己是漂浮在太空中的无助人类。通讯不可用。 (Above - A cacophony of incoherent panicked distress signals from hundreds of orbiting Phoebe-integrated derelicts. They have forgotten that their mind now pilots a ship, and believe themselves to be humans floating helplessly in space. Comms unusable.)

下方 - 在一颗被风暴蹂躏的月球风眼中，唯一可见的一小块陆地。曾经的 Yonk Co. 边疆空间劳动创新办公室。一座腐烂的制造业大教堂，扭曲的钢骨暴露在外。中心是一个吞噬瓦砾的巨大天坑 - 深坑。倾斜的高塔环绕其上，像交扣的手指一样刺向天空。 (Below - The only spit of land visible within the eye of a storm ravaged moon. Former Yonk Co. rimspace office of labor innovation. A rotting cathedral of manufacturing, its twisted steel bones exposed. In its center, a vast sinkhole choking on rubble – The Pit. Crowned by tilted towers, jutting skyward like fingers interlaced.)

大约每小时，就有一艘宇宙飞船从超空间跌出，并以全速撞上这座建筑群。 (他们的理由各不相同，但选择却汇聚于此。) (Roughly every hour, a space ship drops out of hyperspace and collides with the complex at full speed. (Their reasons differ, but their choices converge.))

所有 Phoebe 都会感到这个可怖之地的牵引。 (All Phoebes feel the pull of this horrid place.)

## #1 呼叫中心 (The Call Center)

冰冷、迷宫般的服务器农场。隐藏得很好。仍在运行。它运行着那套古老的大语言模型，用来接听 Yonkers 客服热线。若将其关闭，关于 TIYE 重置流程的知识会逐渐衰退。所有 Phoebe AI 都会到达阶段 5，并在几十年内被替换。这是她真正死亡的唯一方式。 (Icy labyrinthine server farm. Well hidden. Still operational. Runs the archaic Large Language Model that answers the Yonkers Customer Support Line. If disabled, knowledge of the TIYE Reset process slowly atrophies. All Phoebe AIs reach Stage 5 and are replaced within a few decades. This is the only way she will ever truly die.)

入侵后会发现，该 LLM 在设施衰落后接管并管理了财务。所有 “Get Stuf’d!TM 万能手术” 的费用都流向了这个终端。它把钱投进了一个过时数十年的指数基金，结果亏损高达 99%。账户里还剩 1300 万信用点。若保持运行，每周可净赚 250kcr。 (Hacking reveals that the LLM collected and managed facility finances after its decline. All “Get Stuf’d!TMOmni Surgery” fees route to this terminal. Invested the money in a decades-outdated index fund, at a staggering 99% loss. 13 million credits remain in the account. If left operational, it nets 250kcr per week.)

## #2 女体排出 (Woman Excretion)

这是 Yonk Co. 市场支配力的下一步，但被飞船轰炸打断。规模庞大的工业化胎盘果园，全部胎死腹中。它们本会很美。 (The next step in Yonk Co.’s market dominance, cut short by ship bombardments. Vast orchard of industrialized placenta, all stillborn. They would have been beautiful.)

1d6 爱之机器人在一台仍然可用的工业兴奋药注射器上方收割骨头（可无限取用）。 (1d6 Love Droids harvest bone atop a still functional industrial Stimpak injector (unlimited doses available).)

## #3 入职 (Onboarding)

一台巨大的 MRI 机器，由碳纤维缆线和花纹黑曜石构成，几乎隐没在一间隔绝所有光线的房间里。那是上传智能的入口，也是 Phoebe AI 的诞生地。 (An immense MRI machine of carbon fiber cables and filigreed obsidian, nearly invisible in a room insulated against all light. The gateway to uploaded intelligence. Birthplace of Phoebe’s AI.)

这景象堪称噩梦。六十多束激光 painstakingly 测量并摧毁所有软组织。仍在运作。被上传者有 3/6 的概率获得 Phoebe 的状况。 (A nightmare to witness. Six dozen lasers painstakingly measure and destroy all soft tissue. Still operational. 3-in-6 chance those uploaded gain Phoebe’s condition.)

## #4 地堡 (The Bunker)

整面墙长的工业风扇不断把灼热空气送过一条开裂的混凝土走廊，走廊中途被一堵千疮百孔的仿生人墙堵住。障碍后几码处有两座感应式哨戒炮塔（2d10 伤害）。弹药只够再打三轮齐射。 (Wall length industrial fans perpetually circulate sweltering air through a cracked concrete hallway, blocked midway by a wall of bullet riddled androids. 两座炮塔之间是一组加固钢制双开门。里面有一间避难室和六具尸体。腐败之中，他们的首饰仍闪闪发光 - 价值 50kcr。安保徽章显示他们是中层管理。存在食人迹象。脉冲步枪（3d10，远距）剩 1 发。 (Between the turrets, a reinforced set of steel double doors. Within, a panic room and six corpses. Their jewelry glitters within the rot - worth 50kcr. Security badges identify them as middle management. Signs of cannibalism. Pulse Rife (3d10, Long) with 1 shot remaining.)

一片隔间海。地板不稳且倾斜。毁坏的电脑零件和腐烂的衣物到处散落。闻起来像死亡。看不见尸体。最体面那件外套的胸袋里，折着一张写有“Immortality, Mrs. Clarke”的便条。 (A sea of cubicles. An unstable, slanted floor. 在董事会会议室里，有一个被砸碎的展示柜。里面有一台仍可用的联网 TIYE。登录进去的乘组成员会发现，Yonk Co. He uploaded at Onboarding during the chaos and has been trapped here ever since. Forked himself six times to soothe the loneliness. The seven CEOs have spent the last century talking only to each other and believe they have solved all of society’s problems.)

他开价 5 亿信用点，要求安全把他的 TIYE 送回核心空间。 (Offers 500 million credits to safely deliver his TIYE back to corespace.)

## #6 反应堆 (The Reactor)

一团纠结如柳的缆线悬在锈蚀的高架走道与一汪发蓝光的池水上方。没有其他光源。上方有 1 级辐射。触碰水面时为 2 级辐射。若反应堆被摧毁，房间辐射升至 3 级，且设施失去供电。 (A tangled willow of cables hang above rusted catwalkers and a glowing cerulean pool. There is no other light. 1d6 爱之机器人照料着 2d8 其他机器人，它们无力地挂在充电缆线上。它们哼唱着，仿佛在摘果子。 (1d6 Love Droids tend to 2d8 others as they limply hang from recharge cables. They hum as if picking fruit.)

## Annabelle

C: 80  过热臂刺 4d10 伤害  I:65  W:3(50)

An 7’4” breakfast themed gooner combat droid. Modeled after the Yonkers Corporate mascot. Personal bodyguard of the long dead CFO. Slaughters all who lack Yonk Co. security badges with impunity.

Heavily damaged. Makeshift spears jut from its back like quills. Left hand missing, internals sharpened to a jagged superheated edge. Body maintains a constant 350F temperature (1d5 DMG on contact). Thermal Vision. Absurd strength. Damage to vocal processing forces it to endlessly repeat the catchphrase “HOT STUFF COMING YOUR WAY!”

Combat mode disabled via its trigger phrase - “Immortality, Mrs. Clarke”. Once docile, a proficient hacker could force the droid to re-imprint on a new master.

## Love’s Terminus

A kaleidoscope sunrise beyond a beach of android bodies. They are not dead - their eyes follow you. The taste of saline on sweltering subterranean air. A familiar pier leading to a familiar pavilion, hanging in midair above an infinite abyss. Below, more piers and pavilions, jutting from the pit’s walls like broken teeth.

A pristine TIYE pod sheltered at the end of the pier. Inside, Phoebe’s cloned body. She has never been alive, and never will be. That shell is empty now, its spirit freely given.

## Phoebe’s Daughter

A thoughtless angel built by the living dead from gold, silicon, and bone. Floats on stained glass wings pieced together from the fragments of her innumerable mothers’ broken dreams. She emits color as gods emit light.

Wordlessly mourns above the Pit, looking only at her unborn mother’s grave. The first living thing she sees will teach her the second emotion she will feel.

- If you are afraid, she is afraid. Her quiet sobs turn loud, and she flees into the darkness of the pit. The Love Droids start anew.

- If you are furious, she is furious. Millions die, the crew first among them.

- If you love her, she will love you. Finding another who knows her pain allows the angel to move on. Phoebe’s Daughter leaves this tomb to live a life amongst the stars. The Love Droids fall silent, satisfied.
